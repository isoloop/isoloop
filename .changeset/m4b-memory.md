`kernel.inspect()` now shows each isolate's memory, in Chromium on a cross-origin isolated page (ADR 0020, decision 4). The host measures with `performance.measureUserAgentSpecificMemory()` while a reader listens, at most every 5 s, and gives each isolate its worker's bytes as `memory: { bytes, at }`; a pool's members share their worker's figure (`shared: true`). The snapshot carries it and a new `memory` feed message sends it, to `ctx.inspector` too. Chromium answers at its next garbage collection, so a figure is coarse and can be many seconds old. Without cross-origin isolation, and in Firefox and Safari, there is no figure. `@isoloop/vite`'s existing `crossOriginIsolated: true` makes the dev and preview servers send the headers this needs. Each worker now starts from the bootstrap URL with a fragment of its own, which changes nothing that's fetched. `@experimental`.
