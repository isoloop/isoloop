The panel has a Log tab: records as they're appended to the bus, newest first, with the last 200 kept. They come from a tap capped at 50 a second, which runs only while the tab is open and carries on through a bus restart. Topic patterns narrow it; Pause and Clear are in the bar. Values show as 2 KB previews with their size.
