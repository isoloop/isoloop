'@isoloop/devtools': patch

The devtools button is bigger (70 px) and shows the Isoloop mark, in white on a round dark tile with a light edge so it stands out on dark pages. Hover it and two small arrows appear to move it one corner across or up and down. The open panel docks flush against the viewport edge, so it can be dragged to the very bottom of the window instead of stopping 12 px short, and its toolbar has a stretch button (full window width) and a maximize button (the whole window) in place of the corner-switch button; both toggle back, and the choice is kept across reloads. The tab bar stays in view while the views scroll, on a darker background than the frame, with a second level of tabs under it: Pools beside the isolates (when there are pools), and Topics, Subscriptions and Rejected on the Bus view, each with its count. The tables keep their headers in view while the rows scroll, the isolates table has an Actions header, and pools list their members as chips (the long ones fold to "+N more"). The log keeps its toolbar in place and scrolls only its rows, and the panel resizes from its inner corner or from either inner edge. In the log, the time, offset, key and size columns have a fixed width, and a click on a record opens its value indented and coloured, with a Copy button.
