Straight to Failed, no restart

  • render-fmt-mismatch: ready reports a render format the host doesn't speak.
  • runtime-too-old or isolate-too-old: ready.requires is outside the host's [FLOOR, VERSION] (ADR 0012).
  • bus-failed: the bus couldn't load its contracts.
  • Other crashes (error, worker-error, ready-timeout, unresponsive, spawn-failed) restart until 3 restarts within 60 s.

In a pool

  • One beat per worker: members block and are killed together.
  • A worker error or a missed heartbeat crashes every member with that reason; they restart together in a new worker (pool crash).
  • A member's own fatal error, ready timeout or version mismatch stays its own: it restarts alone, back into the same worker.
  • A pool suspends only as a whole, and any member's wake wakes it.

Not a crash

  • A subscription handler that throws reports a non-fatal isolate:error; the isolate keeps running and that subscription stops committing, so its records come back on the next start.
  • Stop and suspend send shutdown; the isolate's bus client sends its last commits and waits for the answer before bye.
  • After a bus restart, suspended headless isolates wake; visual ones wake when seen.
